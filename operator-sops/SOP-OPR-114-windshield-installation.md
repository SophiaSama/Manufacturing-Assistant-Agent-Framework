## 1. Purpose

Define the procedure for adhesive bonding of the front and rear windshields at Station 152 to ensure retention strength and leak-tightness per engineering requirement ENGR-2210.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all operators at Station 152 and to quality inspectors verifying glass installation at Station 168.

## 3. Required Materials

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Surface Preparation
1. Wipe the glass bonding surface with the approved cleaner (isopropyl alcohol, 99%) and allow 2 minutes dry time.
2. Apply primer to the glass edge in a continuous band 10 mm wide. Allow a minimum of 10 minutes open time before adhesive application.
3. Degrease the body aperture flange. Primer is **not** applied to the painted body flange.

### 4.2 Adhesive Application
1. Apply adhesive as a continuous triangular bead, base width **8 mm ± 1 mm**, height **12 mm ± 1 mm**, with no gaps or breaks.
2. Bead path is defined in the station work instruction sheet WIS-152-04. Do not deviate from the path.
3. The maximum open time between adhesive application and glass placement is **10 minutes**. If exceeded, scrap the bead and re-apply.

### 4.3 Glass Placement
1. Position the glass using the vacuum gripper and optical alignment guide.
2. Set the glass with a steady push; do not slide the glass after contact.
3. Confirm flush seating: maximum gap between glass and body flange is 1 mm at any point.

### 4.4 Curing
1. Minimum curing time before any handling of the vehicle: **4 hours at 20–25 °C**.
2. Vehicles are held on the curing line (CV-4) before release to final inspection.
3. If ambient temperature is below 15 °C, curing time extends to 6 hours (winter protocol, see WIS-152-05).

## 5. Quality Requirements

- Leak test: every 10th vehicle undergoes a water-leak test at the soak booth (2 bar, 3 minutes).
- Retention audit: 1 vehicle per shift is destructively tested at the lab; pull strength must exceed **1,200 N** at 7 days cure.
- Any adhesive void larger than 10 mm triggers 100% inspection for that hour's production batch.

## 6. Safety Requirements

- PPE: safety glasses, nitrile gloves (adhesive is skin irritant), approved respirator during priming.
- Adhesive temperature must be 20–30 °C at application. Do not heat adhesive above 60 °C in the pre-heater.
