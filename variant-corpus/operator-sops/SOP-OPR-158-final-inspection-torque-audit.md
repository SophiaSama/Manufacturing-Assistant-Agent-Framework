## 1. Purpose

Define final inspection activities at Station 168, including scheduled torque audits, visual checks, and the disposition of vehicles with findings.

## 2. Scope

Applies to QC inspectors, the final-line supervisor, and quality engineers on all shifts.

## 3. Routine Inspections (Every Vehicle)

| Check | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| Exterior panel gaps | Front door gaps 3.0 mm ± 0.5 mm |
| Windshield seating | Gap ≤ 1 mm, no chip > 3 mm |
| Underbody fasteners (visual) | No loose or missing fasteners |
| Fluid levels (washer, coolant) | Within MIN–MAX |
| Lights, wipers, horn function | Operate correctly |

## 4. Torque Audit Schedule

- Frequency: every **2 hours**, 3 consecutive vehicles per audit.
- Performed with the audit torque wrench **TQ-6018** (TorqMaster TF-6000, calibrated weekly).
- Audited joints:
  1. Wheel lug nuts (5 per wheel, 20 total) — 105 Nm ± 5%
  2. RH engine mount bolts (4) — 62 Nm ± 2 Nm
  3. Rear transmission mount bolts (2) — 45 Nm ± 3 Nm
- Acceptance: **100%** of audited fasteners within tolerance. Any single failure = stop production at the source station, re-audit 10 vehicles, and open a non-conformance (NC) record.

## 5. Audit Failure Handling

1. Stop the line at the source station (144, 118) by calling the shift supervisor.
2. Re-audit the last 10 completed vehicles at the source station.
3. If > 1 of 10 vehicles fails, escalate per ESC-402 Level 3 (Process Engineering) within 2 hours.
4. Document all readings in QDS under audit ID FIN-168.

## 6. Data Recording

- All audit results are entered into QDS in real time. Paper records are not accepted.
- Daily audit summary is reviewed by the Quality Director by 08:00 the next morning.

## 7. Safety Requirements

- PPE: safety glasses, steel-toe boots, hi-vis vest on the line.
- Audit tools must be tethered; never carry loose tools across the line.
- Only authorized personnel may enter the inspection pit.
